# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it the same way.
- Focus on what's best for the project, not on being right.
- Remember that "this code is wrong" and "you are wrong" are different sentences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **lakhwanus009@gmail.com**. Reports will be handled privately and in confidence.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, commits, issues, and PRs that violate this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently for behaviour they consider inappropriate.
